    How to play

    Pass the phone around. Each player reveals their role privately. Innocents see the secret word; impostors see the category, a target, and one hint.

    Discuss for five minutes. Then choose who the group voted out. Catch an impostor and every innocent scores 5 points. If the impostor survives, they score 15 — or 30 if their target was voted out.

    A player can be the impostor twice in a row, then must sit out two played rounds. Skipped rounds do not affect that limit.
